# Changelog

## [0.1.0]

- First release: an MMDVM-compatible network master that puts Trunk Recorder Pro's calls on
  DMR radios through hotspots and repeaters. AMBE+2 voice (P25 Phase 2, DMR, NXDN) goes out
  bit for bit; P25 Phase 1 and ProVoice (IMBE) are converted by their vocoder parameters.
- Slots subscribe to talkgroups on the relay page, in the hotspot's options or by keying up
  from a radio; groups (all calls, each system, your own) play many talkgroups on one slot.
- Talker aliases (the talkgroup on a group, the talker on a talkgroup), a contacts CSV for
  codeplugs, and the recorder's Voice Relay page for its settings with a link to the relay page.
